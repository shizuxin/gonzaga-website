Commission Info

How it works

  1. Check the availability calendar.
  2. Send a booking request with your service, details, and deadline.
  3. Wait for my approval. Your request stays pending until then.
  4. Agree on the final price and payment.
  5. Receive your work and request revisions.

Frequently asked questions

How many revisions are included?

Each commission includes two rounds of revisions. Extra revisions may cost more.

How long does a commission take?

Short edits and graphics usually take 2 to 5 days. Longer videos take more time, so send your deadline early.

How do I pay?

A down payment is needed before I start. The balance is paid before I send the final files.

Can I use the work commercially?

Please tell me your plans in the request. Commercial use may have a different rate.
